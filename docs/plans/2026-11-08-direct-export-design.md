# Direct editing and export, revision 7

The owner rejected revision 6's illustrative homepage, three-stage progress, weak watermark and ambiguous Done action. This revision is a correction of those specific failures.

The owner then explicitly asked for a complete homepage rethink with fewer words and more breathing room. Home now keeps only a compact native brand title, a central scan control and two lightweight Photos/Files actions. The supported list lives behind the native top-right information control. There are no large cards, introduction heading, mock ID, slogan, shield or progress tracker. Lunar's official redesign and Home screenshot informed immediate access to tasks, without copying its banking dashboard, graphics or widgets: https://www.lunar.app/en/personal/new-lunar-experience .

Review supports dragging the full polygon as well as tapping for corner editing. Moving clamps the whole shape to the page, preserving its dimensions and perspective. In the enlarged editor, a drag inside the polygon moves it and a drag outside pans the image. Corner targets remain 44 screen points at every zoom.

The watermark is a compact, lightly angled stamp with distinct label, recipient and context/date sizes, two fine rules and stronger contrast. Every page retains its own burned-in stamp; opaque masks remain opaque. Long input wraps rather than truncates.

The final copy shows JPG/PDF selection and separate Save and Share actions. Save opens the system Files destination picker; Share uses the native activity sheet with the selected format. Close names the act of leaving; it replaces the ambiguous Done. PDF contains only the same flattened JPEG pixels as JPG, arranged on one PDF page (including front/back when present); no source image, text layer, annotations or metadata dictionary is included. Format selection does not re-run recognition or render the source.
