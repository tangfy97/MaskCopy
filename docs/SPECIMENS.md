# Test images

Maske is tested on three kinds of images. Real people's documents are never among them.

| Kind | Where | Committed? |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic documents | Drawn by `maske fixtures` from `Sources/maske/Fixtures.swift` | The generator only; images are regenerated by `scripts/check.sh` |
| PRADO specimens | The Council of the EU's register of identity documents | No (© Council of the EU) |
| Wikimedia Commons specimens | `scripts/fetch_specimens.py` | No (various licences; the manifest records each) |

Everything downloaded goes in `Fixtures/specimens/` (git-ignored). Generated folders (`Fixtures/synthetic/`, `Fixtures/warped-*`) are git-ignored too.

## Synthetic documents

41 documents with made-up people and valid check digits: passports (NOR, NLD, DEU, USA, GBR, CHN, JPN, KOR, AUS, SGP), European ID cards front and back (NLD, DEU, BEL, ITA, ESP, POL, SWE), a Norwegian residence card, Asian ID cards (CN, HK, TW, KR, SG, MY), a Japanese residence card, driving licences (NLD, NOR, FRA, GBR, NZL, USA front and back with PDF417, CAN Ontario, AUS NSW, CHN, JPN, KOR, SGP, MYS).

Each is drawn on a dark "table" with margins and rounded corners, so finding the document is part of the test. `expected.json` holds, for every field, its role (`hide` with a kind, or `keep`) and its exact ink bounds (from Core Text's glyph image bounds), in the coordinates of the whole image. The portraits are drawn silhouettes, which Vision does not see as faces.

To add one: add a `Doc` in `Fixtures.all`, mark text with `.hide(kind)` or `.keep`, and write `"Label |value"` when only the value is to be hidden.

## PRADO (Public Register of Authentic identity and travel Documents Online)

<https://www.consilium.europa.eu/prado/> covers all EU and EEA countries and Switzerland, and many others, including every supported country (USA, CAN, AUS, NZL, CHN, HKG, TWN, JPN, KOR, SGP, MYS, GBR).

Structure (as of November 2026):
- Country page: `/prado/en/prado-documents/<ISO3>/index.html`
- Category list, ordinary documents: `/prado/en/prado-documents/<ISO3>/<category>/O/docs-per-type.html`. Categories: **A** passport, **B** identity card, **H** residence-related document, **F** driving licence (C visa, T and J travel documents, I seafarer's).
- Document page: `/prado/en/<ISO3>-<cat><type>-<number>/index.html`, e.g. `NLD-FO-07001` (newest first in the lists).
- Images: `/prado/images/<document code>/<image id>.jpg`. The first two image links after each document in a list are its front ("recto") and back ("verso"), about **600 px wide**: far smaller than a phone photo, so text recognition on them is a harsh test.
- Cookie banner: choose "I accept only necessary cookies".

PRADO's bot protection rejects command-line downloads. **Don't work around it** (no fake browser identities). Collect through a normal browser instead:

1. `python3 -I scripts/prado_receiver.py Fixtures/specimens/prado`
2. Open the search page in a browser, accept only necessary cookies, open the developer console and paste `scripts/prado_collect.js`.
3. Allow the browser's prompt to reach a device on the local network (the receiver on 127.0.0.1:8765). Progress is in `window.__prado`.

The collection of 7 November 2026 (about an hour): 745 images, the two newest ordinary versions of categories A, B, H and F for 63 countries (all of Europe, North America, Oceania, East and Southeast Asia, India, Brazil). File names: `<ISO3>-<category>-<document code>-<front|back>.jpg`.

## Wikimedia Commons

```sh
python3 -I scripts/fetch_specimens.py Fixtures/specimens/commons
```

Uses the Commons API with a descriptive user agent, one request at a time. It searches "<country> <document> specimen" for each country and document kind and keeps files whose title or description says specimen or sample. `manifest.json` records title, source page, licence and author for each file.

**Known problem:** the search also matched botanical "specimens" (herbarium envelopes), so of 147 files many are not documents. Before using them, filter by document words in the title/description (passport, identity, ID card, licence, permis, Ausweis, 护照, 身份证…) or look through them.

## Other sources to consider

- AAMVA's DL/ID card design standard (sample US and Canadian layouts, PDF417 contents).
- Government pages with specimen images (e.g. Rijksoverheid for Dutch documents, gov.uk for the UK licence).
- Photographing printed specimens with an iPhone at an angle, in ordinary light: the closest thing to real use.
