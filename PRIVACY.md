# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

MaskCopy is an iPhone app for sharing a copy of an identity document with parts of it hidden, made by TangSpark.

**MaskCopy collects no data.** Its code contains no networking, and it has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or third-party code. Unlike a Mac app, an iPhone app cannot be barred from the internet by the system, so this is a promise about MaskCopy's code, which is open source for anyone to check.

What MaskCopy does on your iPhone:

- It uses the camera only while you scan a document, and photos and files only when you choose them.
- It reads the document with Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone to find the parts to hide: text, faces and barcodes. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.
- It keeps the document and its preview in memory while you review it. It writes a temporary JPG or PDF only when you choose Save or Share. Save opens the system Files picker; the copy remains at the destination you choose until you delete it. Temporary working copies are deleted when you finish, leave the document, start again, or next open MaskCopy. It keeps no history.
- The copy it makes has the hidden parts painted over with an opaque fill. The recipient, purpose and date form one diagonal watermark inside every document page. It uses dark text over the document and light text over opaque masks. JPG and PDF contain only the flattened copy, with no source photo metadata such as location or camera details. PDF contains a single image page, without text layers, annotations or a metadata dictionary.
- While a document is open, MaskCopy covers it when you switch apps, so it doesn't show in the app switcher.

Where the copy goes after you share it is up to you and the app you share it with.

MaskCopy is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Questions: [support@tang-spark.no](mailto:support@tang-spark.no).
