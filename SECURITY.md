# Security

Maske handles identity documents, so anything that could leave a sensitive part visible is taken seriously.

If you find a security issue, please email **hello@tang-spark.no** instead of opening a public issue. Include the steps to reproduce it and the versions of Maske and iOS. **Never send a photo of a real document**; describe it, or use a specimen published by its issuing authority. You will get a reply within a few days, and a fix will be released as soon as it is ready.

Most reports will be about the copy: a personal number, document number, barcode, photo or machine-readable zone left visible; a mask that can be removed or seen through; metadata left in the shared file; or the document being kept after sharing.
