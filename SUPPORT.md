# MaskCopy support

MaskCopy makes a copy of your passport, ID card, residence permit or driving licence with the sensitive parts covered, entirely on your iPhone.

**Contact:** [support@tang-spark.no](mailto:support@tang-spark.no). We reply within a few days.

**Never send a photo of a real identity document**, not even a masked one. Tell us the country, the kind of document and roughly when it was issued, and which field was covered wrongly or left visible.

## Common questions

**A number or the photo wasn't covered.** Tap the + button on the review screen to add a box, or tap an existing box to move it or drag its corners. Every box can be adjusted before you share. Please tell us which document it was, so the next version recognises it.

**Something is covered that doesn't need to be.** Switch that kind off in the list under the document, or tap the box and turn off Hide.

**Which documents are supported?** Open the information button on the first screen. Passports, national ID cards, residence permits and driving licences from 27 countries are recognised field by field. Passports from other countries still have their machine-readable zone found. Recognition varies by document version and photo, so check every copy before sharing.

**Does MaskCopy upload my document?** No. It has no networking code, no account and no analytics. The document is read on your iPhone and nothing is kept after you finish. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Where is my saved copy?** Save puts it where you chose in the Files app. Share hands it to the app you pick. MaskCopy keeps no copy itself.

Security issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
